In [my article on my faith journey so far](/journey), I argued that every Bible-believing Christian must acknowledge the two decisive movements of God in the twentieth century.

The first was the **outpouring of the Holy Spirit**: not a doctrine but a happening, supernatural and tangible at once. From Wales in 1904 to Azusa Street in 1906 to Pyongyang in 1907, the **latter rain** fell on a church that had not seen its like for centuries — tongues, healings, signs, the Book of Acts breaking out again in tin-roofed halls and coal-mining chapels.

The second was a nation. On 14 May 1948, **Israel was born in a day**, exactly as Isaiah said a country would be.

These are not two moves of God. They are one move, seen from two ends: the Spirit who fell on Wales and Azusa and Pyongyang is the Spirit who prayed a nation back into its land. That is the argument I made there, and the life of **Rees Howells** is where it stops being an argument and becomes a diary.

## The bridge

Rees Howells (1879–1950) was a Welsh miner. **The fire he received in the Welsh Revival became the flame in the engine room of his prayer for all nations.** He learned to pray with persistence, and he learned to pray the way Nehemiah prayed — by reminding God of what God had already [promised](/promises). It is the most powerful prayer we have: **not a new argument, but God’s own word handed back to Him.**

> “Remember, I pray, the word that You commanded Your servant Moses, saying, ‘If you are unfaithful, I will scatter you among the nations; but if you return to Me, and keep My commandments and do them, though some of your outcasts are in the uttermost parts of heaven, from there I will gather them and bring them to the place which I have chosen to make My name dwell there.’”
>
> Nehemiah 1:8–9, NKJV

In 1924 he founded the **Bible College of Wales** in Swansea, and the college became **a house of prayer**: Hitler, Mussolini, the Ethiopian emperor, the whole map of the war, prayed over one crisis at a time.

Then, in October and November 1947, the burden changed. Students laid down their missionary calls and stayed to pray for one thing: **the return of the Jews to the land**. Howells wrote in his diary:

> “We pleaded that because of His covenant with Abraham 4,000 years ago, God would take His people back to their land.”

They were not praying a sentiment. **They were reminding God of a promise**, which is the prayer of Nehemiah — **not a new request, but an old word held up before God.**

On the night of the vote, one of the students later recalled, they saw **the angels of God around the UN building** and knew that no delegation could thwart the plan of God for His own people.

On **29 November 1947** the United Nations voted. **Thirty-three for, thirteen against, ten abstentions** — and among the thirty-three were the United States, the Soviet Union, Poland and Czechoslovakia. It is one of the very few moments in the whole Cold War when Washington and Moscow wanted the same thing.

The college called that day **“one of the greatest days for the Holy Ghost in the history of these 2,000 years.”**

Not one of the greatest days for politics. For the Holy Ghost. They knew what they had been praying to.

So the same Spirit who fell in the coal mines of Wales in 1904 and on Azusa Street in 1906 prayed a nation back into existence in 1947. **The revival and the restoration were the same breath.** The outpouring and the fig tree are one move with two halves, and the men carrying the first half were praying for the second.

## The other move

Here is a verse many Christians do not even know is in the Bible:

> “We know that we are of God, and that **the whole world lies in the power of the evil one**.”
>
> 1 John 5:19, NASB

**The world — not a corner of it.** Thrones, markets, universities, screens, the air people breathe. John does not say it lies under judgement yet. It lies in his power now.

The wilderness proves it. The devil took Jesus up on an exceedingly high mountain, showed Him all the kingdoms of the world and their glory, and put them on the table:

> “All these things I will give You if You will fall down and worship me.”
>
> Matthew 4:8–9, NKJV

**He could make that offer because the world was his to give.** No creature offers what he does not hold. Those kingdoms were his by legal right — **Adam’s dominion, forfeited at the Fall and handed over with it**. And Jesus did not argue the claim. **He refused the price.**

So if God moved twice in the last century, ask the obvious question: did the evil one stand still? **If the two moves of God were really one, the two moves of Satan are one as well.** And both of them are aimed at a single target.

## First: red and green

The first is the alliance we now call **Red-Green**.

The Red is the post-modern liberal neo-Marxism that lost the factory floor and took the faculty instead: **the long march through the institutions**, one at a time — universities, then media, then civil service, then police, then the church. I have written about [why Christians must reject Leftism](/leftism), and about how [multiculturalism](/multiculturalism) became its delivery vehicle. The mechanism matters less than the fact that the march is finished.

The Green is Islam: **fourteen centuries of conquest jihad**, from Arabia into Egypt and North Africa, through the Levant and Anatolia and Persia, across the sea into Spain — and, in our own lifetime, **a migration jihad into Western Europe**, arriving not with an army in the field but with a claim on the welfare state and a demand for [halal](/halal) recognition. I have argued elsewhere that [our asylum policy](/asylum) was designed by people who cannot say the word “Islam.”

The two should despise each other. One is materialist, the other theological; one wants the sexual revolution, the other wants the veil. Yet they march together, and they always have. The prototype came in **Iran in 1979**: the left supplied the strikes, the students, the slogans and the world’s sympathy, and the mosque supplied the crowd. Khomeini let them carry the revolution and then hanged them from the cranes. [I have written about where that regime is heading](/iran).

So why does this alliance hold? Because they hate the same three things: **the God of Israel, the nation-state, and the family.**

## Second: the replacement

The second move is quieter, and it is inside the house.

**Replacement Theology** — the doctrine that **the church has replaced Israel**, that **the promises to Abraham now belong to us**, that the Jews are a discarded draft of a cancelled plan. It did not arrive last week. Origen allegorised the literal Israel away. Augustine built the city of God on the ruin of the earthly one. Luther, for all his recovery of justification, carried the same eraser into the Reformation and wrote it into a 1543 tract that the Nazis would later quote.

**From Augustine to Auschwitz.** That is not rhetoric; it is a road. A doctrine that told Europe for **fifteen hundred years** that God was finished with the Jews prepared the ground for a century that decided to finish them. The gas chambers were not built by theologians. They were built by a civilisation the theologians had taught to despise the people of the promise.

And the eraser is in the pulpit this Sunday, in churches that would never march for Hamas. Any sermon that says God has no future with the Jewish nation has taken the second move for him.

## The crux

Now put the two side by side.

The Red-Green sword says: **eliminate them.**
The replacement pen says: **erase them.**

Different tools. Same target: **Israel, the people of God**. And behind both, one mind.

Why? Because of one sentence Jesus left us.

> “For I say to you, you shall see Me no more till you say, ‘Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord!’”
>
> Matthew 23:39, NKJV

> “Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord! We have blessed You from the house of the Lord.”
>
> Psalm 118:26, NKJV

Jesus tied His second coming to a word spoken by Jerusalem. Not to the church’s programmes, not to the world’s improvement, but to a national recognition: **you will not see Me until you say it.** The day Israel says _Baruch Haba B’Shem Adonai_, the heavens open.

So the enemy’s arithmetic writes itself. **No Israel, no welcome. No welcome, no return.** No return, no [throne of David](/throne-of-david), no Millennial Kingdom — and the usurper keeps the world he already holds.

Now look at his two moves and ask what they are for. **The sword would take the Jews off the earth; the doctrine would take them out of the story.** **Either way the people of God are gone** — and that is the whole point. **_Satan cannot reach the throne, so he goes for the nation standing in front of it._**

Two things drive him. First, they are the people God has bound His own name to:

> “For thus says the Lord of hosts: ‘He sent Me after glory, to the nations which plunder you; for he who touches you touches the apple of His eye.’”
>
> Zechariah 2:8, NKJV

So hurting them is how he gets back at God, by going for the very thing God treasures. Second, they are the lock on the door of Christ’s return: **remove the people and you remove the welcome, and the King has no nation to come back to.** That is why antisemitism is not merely an ethnic prejudice but **a deeply spiritual phenomenon**. It is not hatred of a people for its own sake; it is an attempt to stop the return of the King.

But both of his doors are bolted from the other side, and Paul shows us the bolts.

> “I say then, has God cast away His people? Certainly not! For I also am an Israelite, of the seed of Abraham, of the tribe of Benjamin. God has not cast away His people whom He foreknew.”
>
> Romans 11:1–2, NKJV

That one sentence is the funeral of Replacement Theology. If God has not cast away His people, then no council, no reformer and no pulpit has the authority to do it for Him. And Paul does not leave it there:

> “For I do not desire, brethren, that you should be ignorant of this mystery, lest you should be wise in your own opinion, that blindness in part has happened to Israel until the fullness of the Gentiles has come in. And so all Israel will be saved, as it is written: ‘The Deliverer will come out of Zion, and He will turn away ungodliness from Jacob.’”
>
> Romans 11:25–26, NKJV

**Until.** That word is a deadline, and the devil reads the same Bible we do. He is not trying to win the argument; he is trying to arrive before the door opens. **His days are numbered, and that is what makes him furious.**

## The twist

Now the part that turns all of this into confidence rather than dread.

**Nothing the enemy does escapes God’s permission, and nothing he wins is outside God’s plan.** Joseph said it to the brothers who had sold him:

> “But as for you, you meant evil against me; but God meant it for good, in order to bring it about as it is this day, to save many people alive.”
>
> Genesis 50:20, NKJV

The cross is the pattern. The worst thing men have ever done — the murder of God — was the thing God had determined beforehand to do for us. Wicked hands and a settled purpose, in the same act.

So look at 1948 and tell me the devil was winning. Theodor Herzl had been arguing for a Jewish state since 1896, and for fifty years the world treated him as a crank with a pamphlet. Then came the camps. Six million murdered, and with them the last shred of the world’s confidence in its own civilisation. **Humanly speaking, without the Holocaust there is no vote on 29 November 1947.** The nations that had turned refugee ships away could not afterwards vote no. The state was born out of ashes the enemy made. **He meant it for evil; God meant it for good, and the good was a nation.**

The Red-Green alliance will be the same. It is the clearest precursor we have to **the Man of Sin** and his world government — one system, one authority, one demanded worship, a world already trained to call it peace. And it is also the pressure that will finally do what two thousand years of preaching has not. Zechariah says God will pour on Jerusalem a spirit of grace and supplication, and they will look on Him whom they pierced. **The siege becomes the summons. The pressure becomes the prayer.**

That is why we pray and intercede and cry out to God for His people with not one ounce of despair. **Our God is the God of Romans 8:28**:

> “And we know that all things work together for good to those who love God, to those who are the called according to His purpose.”
>
> Romans 8:28, NKJV

**The devil is not the author of this story.** He is a character in it, and he is being written into an ending he did not choose.

## Watch

[The fig tree is in leaf](/end3). The rain is still falling. And **the lock the enemy is trying to break** has been in the land for **seventy-eight years**, with the covenants, the promises and the calling still attached to it:

> “Concerning the gospel they are enemies for your sake, but concerning the election they are beloved for the sake of the fathers. For the gifts and the calling of God are irrevocable.”
>
> Romans 11:28–29, NKJV

**We are not spectators in this. We are the watchmen.** The church that prays for the peace of Jerusalem is not being sentimental about a foreign country; it is praying for the return of her King. So stay awake. Read the prophets. Pray for the Jews — not as a policy, but as a people God has not finished with. Paul prayed it before us:

> “Brethren, my heart’s desire and prayer to God for Israel is that they may be saved.”
>
> Romans 10:1, NKJV

**He is not late. He is not blocked.** He is coming, and He has told us what has to be said first.

**Maranatha.**
