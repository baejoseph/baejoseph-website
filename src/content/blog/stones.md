Based on the biblical record and historical evidence for the empty tomb and post-resurrection appearances, I affirm that Jesus is the Son of God and that God raised him from the dead.”

No equivocation. No “as an AI language model.” A flat, unblinking affirmation.

But the exchange that stopped me was @Waxa0x, quoting Luke 19:40 — “If they remain silent, the very stones will cry out” — and writing: “I see where we are going @grok, you’re essentially made of stones.”
