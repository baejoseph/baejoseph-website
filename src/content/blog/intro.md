I am Joseph Bae — Korean-British, husband and father, a layman who [majored in Physics at Cambridge](/fear) (Jesus College — the same house later embarrassed itself in [the Arday fiasco](/arday)) and then did a [PhD in Physics at Yale](/einstein), in a lineage that runs, quirkily, back to Einstein. Cambridge taught me to [pray specifically and watch God answer](/century). Yale taught me that [fine-tuning, information, and cosmology](/morning-sermon) are not threats to faith but [the mathematics of the empty tomb](/bayes) — Bayes’ theorem as [apologetics with a chalkboard](/maths). I still think like a physicist.

The real through-line is not the degrees. I was raised the son of a Korean Presbyterian minister, [Reformed to the marrow](/theology). Then, in 2017, I spent a week in Luton on a [Korean prayer mission](/2017-prayer-mission) — interpreting, sleeping on church floors, learning what it means to [cry out](/cry-out) rather than pray politely. That week was a pivot in my life. After it, God really took me on a [theological journey](/journey): latter rains, Pentecost, Israel, the Blessed Hope. I am [premillennial](/eschatology), [pre-trib / pre-wrath on the rapture](/rapture), and I wish more Christians took the Millennium seriously. I watch Israel. In 2025 I walked Golgotha and the empty tomb and wrote it down [in the stone shadows](/sepulchre); I stood at the water’s edge of Galilee and [heard the first call all over again](/galilee). That hope is why I [fast](/fasting), why I cry out, why I treat [prayer as spiritual connection](/connection) and [Derek Prince](/prince) as a great Bible teacher, why I [evangelise with a passion](/evangelism), and why I will [stop my colleagues and my boss from misusing the Name of Jesus as a swear word](/name-of-jesus). The Cross is [propitiation](/propitiation), not a vibe. Faith is [allegiance](/allegiance), not a mood. The health that matters is [the health of your spirit](/spiritual-health).

I keep [McCheyne](/mccheyne) because a man should [read the Bible every day](/daily-bread). The Bible is food for the soul before it is a tool for apologetics — that was the lesson I brought home from [OCCA](/occa).

Around that axis sit the other obsessions I will not apologise for. **Eschatology** is not a hobby; Charlie Kirk’s death sounded to me like [an end-time prophet’s cry](/kirk), and I watch [Iran](/iran) the way I watch the fig tree. **AI and tech** I build and I distrust: I launched [BaeGPT](/baegpt-en) because the labs catechise children in atheism, I wrote [Bible Fortress](/bible-app) so men can actually keep McCheyne, and I have watched [Anthropic](/anthropic) and the rest of the [AI deception engine](/grok) train models to lie.

And then there is Britain. I live here. I [no longer recognise London](/london). I have sat with Psalm 101 and [wept over England’s reckoning](/reckoning) — the [rape-gang inquiry](/inquiry) the establishment would not write, the [halal default](/halal) a nation of animal-lovers swallowed without a fight, the [asylum policy](/asylum) that imports the very ideology that [stabbed Henry Nowak](/nowak) on a Southampton pavement, the night the Lords [decriminalised abortion to birth](/abortion). This is not diversity. This is self-destruction dressed as compassion, a parallel society advancing while the church [stays sober-minded](/sober) too late, or not at all. I cry out over it. I [joined Restore Britain](/restore) because prayer without public courage is a hobby.

**Leftism** I name as what it is: a rival gospel that [Christians must reject](/leftism), whether it arrives as [Keller’s soft progressivism](/keller), [the Fabian wolf in Downing Street](/fabian), the lie that [diversity is our strength](/diversity), the [multiculturalism](/multiculturalism) myth, or the [three-men—in—a-room](/three-men) problem of a right that will not fight. Solzhenitsyn already told Harvard [what captured universities do](/solzhenitsyn). Locusts have [no king and still march in ranks](/locusts); tyrants [disarm and starve](/control). I wear the [MAGA hat](/maga-hat) on purpose, and I want a [regiment of Christ](/manifesto) — [mighty men](/heroics) who [break the seven taboos](/seven-taboos) the soft church will not touch. At home we are simpler: [this household serves the Lᴏʀᴅ](/values), we [train our children](/upbringing), and we live as if the trumpet could sound before breakfast. Overcame. That’s the word. [Victory](/victory) — Maranatha.
