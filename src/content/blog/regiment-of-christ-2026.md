# Regiment of Christ 2026

**A Manifesto for the Regiment: Testosterone-Filled Men of God for the Last-Hour Harvest**

Imagine it: a tight band of twenty like-minded, Spirit-filled, testosterone-charged young men of God. Not soft, not recent converts, not weekend warriors—but a living *regiment*, shoulder-to-shoulder in the local church, carrying the battle honours of those who fought addiction, held marriages, raised sons in the faith, and refused to break when the world pressed hard. They are the covenant unit God raises in this [end of the end times](/end3/) to supercharge the final harvest. They fight for the man beside them, make their regiment proud, and advance the Kingdom with unyielding masculine glory. As the British Army regiments teach us, loyalty to this small, historic band—rooted in the cloud of witnesses who subdued kingdoms and stopped lions’ mouths—turns abstract faith into concrete warfare. This is no private spirituality. This is formation. This is how Christian men are forged and how the Kingdom advances.

Such men are rare. They must meet ironclad requirements, drawn straight from Scripture and the hard-won testimony of faithful brothers.

They must **not be recent converts**, lest they be lifted up with pride and fall into the condemnation of the devil (1 Timothy 3:6). Novices lack the tested depth to lead without ego destroying the regiment.

They must **know the Bible**—every book, read cover-to-cover at least ten times, serious students who memorize large sections (Psalms 27, 34, 101, 118, 119, 139, 144, 145; Matthew 5–7) to meditate daily. The Word is daily bread: Old Testament once and New Testament plus Psalms twice per year, a lamp to the feet and light to the path ([Psalm 119:105](/daily-bread/)). It anchors life through trials, teaches the fear of the Lord, and prepares us for Christ’s quick return (Revelation 22:12). Technology serves the Kingdom here—simple, distraction-free tools like a native iOS app delivering M’Cheyne readings with clean NKJV text and high-quality audio prove men can build what feeds the Church. No clutter, just Scripture. Feast on the Word; starve the flesh.

They must **be filled with the Holy Spirit and regularly practice the gifts**. The [journey of faith](/journey/) moves from solid Reformed roots—guarding truth against feminism, humanism, [cultural Marxism](/leftism-eng/), and the sexual revolution—to embracing the latter rains: Azusa Street, Welsh Revival, Pyongyang 1907. These outpourings of tongues, signs, wonders, healing, and prophecy bookend the Church Age (Joel 2:23; Mark 16:17–18). The Spirit is not optional decoration; He is power for the harvest. Men who walk this journey refuse to let the gifts lie dormant while the world burns.

They must have a **proven record of standing firm**, holding fast to Biblical truths amid real adversity. They confront the misuse of Jesus’ name as a curse in workplaces—bosses, colleagues—and demand reverence, earning respect and behavioral change (Matthew 10:32). They lead early-morning prayer, memorize Scripture under pressure, and publicly confess Christ whether in Korean corporate dinners or Western offices. They refuse to let cultural softness or PC norms silence them. These are the heroics: David’s mighty men—Issachar who [understood the times](/heroics/), Gadites swift as gazelles, faces like lions—who smelled of blood, sweat, and covenant loyalty. They act like men (1 Corinthians 16:13), go on the offensive, cast out demons, and reject the castrated, feelings-first religion that has infected the Church. Young men starve for this tribe, this mission, this King.

They must **understand the true nature of leftism** and how it has overtaken the world. Leftism is not mere politics; it is satanic. It diagnoses the world’s problem as inequality (not sin) and prescribes utopian revolution by dismantling family, religion, private property, and created order—exactly the agenda of cultural Marxism, critical theory, and the “woke” ethos that has captured academia, media, seminaries, and culture. It violates the Eighth Commandment (property) and Tenth (covetousness). Anti-communism (반공) is not enough. It demands the 멸공 spirit—total destruction of this worldview root and branch. Tyrants have always used the same handbook: disarm the people (guns, Second Amendment as porcupine defense) and starve them (poison diets, seed oils, chained Bibles, captured media). The Church must refuse to be disarmed spiritually or physically. Leftism and its soft-Christian cousins are the same poison. We scorn it, mock it, and raise David’s banner instead.

Their **eschatology must be well-considered and [pre-millennial](/rapture/)**. This is non-negotiable—it reveals a literal hermeneutic that honors Scripture’s plain reading of Revelation 20. Jesus returns *before* the [thousand-year reign](/throne-of-david/) to rule from David’s throne. Amillennial or postmillennial views quietly erode urgency, treating the return as remote or symbolic and risking the error of “immanentizing the eschaton” (trying to build utopia by human effort, functionally identical to Marxism). Pre-millennialism keeps the sharp edge: the world grows darker, justice comes only when [King Jesus](/values/) returns with a rod of iron.

That eschatology must **fuel urgency** in every realm—evangelism and discipleship (church realm), civic resistance to leftism and communism (civic realm), and eager waiting for the glorious appearing, [Millennial Kingdom](/courage/), and New Jerusalem (eternal realm). We preach, push back, and labor faithfully, knowing ultimate victory is His. No human effort restores the world before He comes.

They must **understand the power of technology** and harness it passionately for Kingdom purposes, with a proven record of building. Whether launching Bible apps, calendar tools, or clean platforms for daily bread, these men refuse to let tech serve only Babylon. They build what feeds the saints, spreads the Word, and equips the harvest.

Most importantly, they must be **men of [prayer and fasting](/the-prince/)**. They cry out corporately—loud, simultaneous, raw “Abba! Father!” or “주여!”—shifting atmospheres as in Acts 4 and Korean *[tongseong gido](/connected/)*. Faith itself is the courage to cry out in affliction: “I believed, therefore I spoke: I am greatly afflicted” (2 Corinthians 4:13; Psalm 116:10). They fast 21 days (or more), feasting on Scripture and memorized Psalms while the body wastes fat and the spirit sharpens. Fasting is the Church’s thermonuclear weapon—humility, breakthrough, answered prayer—for end-times power. Jesus assumed we would fast; the regiment obeys.

Such men will change the world. They are the last-hour workers who supercharge the harvest while the world spirals. They eat meat, lift weights, read the Bible, fast with purpose, pray like warriors, and refuse every form of disarming softness.

O Lord of the harvest, send forth these workers! Raise the regiment. Make Your men proud. Maranatha—come quickly, Lord Jesus. The fields are white. The [time is short](/iran/). Let the mighty men arise.
