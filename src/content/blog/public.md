On the 8th of October the Pentagon announced it will livestream the firing-squad execution of Nidal Malik Hasan, the Fort Hood shooter. Pete Hegseth, the Secretary of War, said he wanted it public. God bless him for not flinching. It will be the first American military execution in over sixty years. And, predictably, the liberal leftists and the [mainstream-media globalist elite](/sober) are up in arms, “horrified.”

But let’s stop here for a moment and think.

Those same people who are up in arms now cheered the video of [Charlie Kirk’s assassination](/kirk) a year ago. They shared it, parodied it, enjoyed it. And now that a public execution finally comes around for a man who cruelly killed thirteen, suddenly it is “horrifying.”

In August 2025 we watched, in the open, the public killing of a Ukrainian woman living quietly in America, Iryna Zarutska. On her light-rail train home from work, in broad daylight, a Black man sitting behind her stabbed her several times with a knife, *solely because she was white.* A career criminal, convicted again and again, yet set free every single time because of his skin. The video of her dying was shock itself.

There is more. [Henry Nowak](/nowak), just eighteen, was stabbed to death on a night street in Southampton, and the police who handcuffed the dying boy told him, “You think you’ve been stabbed? Don’t lie.” The man who hurt him, and his family, told the police “he’s a racist!”—and with that one word, the killer became the victim and the victim was processed as the criminal.

And one more thing that cannot be left out. The Pakistani Muslim gangs that have, for decades, openly raped at least 250,000 white British girls across every corner of the UK—[the inquiry](/inquiry), the [reckoning](/reckoning), [Restore Britain](/restore)—most of them are still walking freely down British streets today.

We live in a society that is entirely back to front.

> “For the ruler is not a terror for the good, but for the evil. Do you wish to have no fear of the authority? Do what is good, and you will have praise from the authority, for he is God’s servant for your good. But if you do what is wrong, be afraid, for he does not bear the sword in vain; for he is God’s servant, an avenger to bring punishment on the one who does wrong.”

Romans 13:3–4

That is the state’s raison d’être. It is not there to make citizens feel comfortable. It is a sword. To punish the wicked—publicly, in the open, where everyone can see. That is not a corruption of the state. It is the point of it.

And Deuteronomy 13 makes the purpose plain:

> “You shall certainly kill him. Your hand shall be first to put him to death, and afterwards the hand of all the people. You shall certainly stone him. So that all Israel shall hear, and be afraid, and do no more any such thing.”

The public execution is not merely justice. It is the deterrent that God allowed, so that when it happens again the whole people shall hear, and be afraid, and never do such a thing.

And I am assured of this: there will be public executions, however rare, in the Millennial Kingdom. [Revelation 20:4](/eschatology) shows the saints reigning with Christ—that thousand-year reign will be neither democracy nor today’s liberalism. The wicked will not be quietly re-educated. Justice will flow like a river.

As of 2026, our society has grown too weak, too soft. Too effeminate. We need more testosterone—to act like men.

[Repeal the 19th](/control). [Bring back justice](/courage).

Maranatha.
