On 29 October 2026, a single frame went up with four words across the top: *The White male experience*. A boy stands alone in the middle of a circle of Jedi elders, high above a city with no edge. He is nine. He was a slave last week. Every seat in that room carries a rank. He carries none.

That is the entire meme. Within days people were calling it the most culturally significant post of the year.

![A boy standing alone in a circle of seated Jedi elders, captioned “The White male experience”](/assets/anakin-white-male-experience.jpg)

![A quote-post calling “The White male experience” the most culturally significant post of 2026](/assets/anakin-culturally-significant.jpg)

In this post, we will explore why this meme based on the Star Wars prequel movies has so captured the zoomer generation.

## Who he is

In *The Phantom Menace*, Anakin Skywalker is a child slave in a junk shop on Tatooine, a desert at the edge of the map, when Qui-Gon Jinn and Obi-Wan Kenobi, two Jedi knights on a secret mission, cross paths with him. When Qui-Gon tests Anakin’s blood, the Force is thicker in him than in Yoda. The old prophecy calls such a one the Chosen One. But when Qui-Gon presents him to the Jedi Council (the above picture), they reject Anakin on the grounds that he is too old, too afraid, and too attached to his mother.

Ten years on, Anakin is the most gifted knight of his generation. He loves Padmé. He dreams of building a family with her. Then one day, Anakin dreams his mother back on Tatooine is dying. He goes back to find her taken by the Sand People, and watches her die in his arms in a tent.

He kills them all. Men, women, children. *They’re like animals, and I slaughtered them like animals.* And so begins his descent to the dark side, according to the prequel movies.

## The hearing

Let’s break down the Council hearing. In the meme version, the Council informs a former slave that he benefited from systemic racism because he is a white male:

![The Council informs a former slave that he benefited from systemic racism because he is a white male](/assets/anakin-systemic-racism.jpg)

Born a slave. Desert planet. No father. But still the beneficiary of an unjust system by reason of his identity: white, male, and heterosexual. The charge is categorical — what he *is*, not what he did. A ring of diverse elders (a cosmic-scale DEI), sitting around one human child, rules that he is guilty by the color of his skin and the chromosome of his DNA. This is what the meme is ridiculing.

![The Council rules that wanting a home, a wife and children before forty is far-right extremism](/assets/anakin-far-right-extremism.jpg)

The next meme widens the charge. A home. A wife. Children before forty. The means to provide for them. The Council rules that this is far-right extremism. Anakin answers that he just wants to provide for his family. The Council has already decided what is best for his family. The ridiculousness is the point. The current woke 2.0 is ripe for mockery and ridicule.

This is also when the meme expands to generational struggle: boomers against the zoomers.

![Luke as the entitled boomer, Anakin as the average zoomer](/assets/anakin-boomer-zoomer.jpg)

On the left, Luke, from the original trilogy, released in the 1970s. The chart calls him the average boomer: a job with no experience, a private island, and nothing passed on to the next generation. He is the golden boy who rode the great boom, the 401k multiplying on the S&P. On the right, Anakin, from the prequels, released in the 2000s. The chart calls him the average zoomer: the best employee, still not promoted, under a boss who is nine hundred years old and will not retire. Yoda is literally nine hundred. The people who hand out those promotions, the powers that be, are token diversity hires with nothing to show for it but [incompetence](/arday/).

![Obi-Wan offers the house, the high ground and the pension; Anakin says no](/assets/anakin-high-ground-house.jpg)

*It’s over, Anakin. Just let me sit in my million-dollar house and collect Social Security out of your paycheck.* The second face is the whole answer. *No.*

## The mother

The Sand People raped and killed his mother. The meme asks what the respectable reply was, and puts it in the mouth of the friend. Obi-Wan loves him and talks like a press office. The scolding comes from the man who should have been with him. Four pictures. Four lines of woke 2.0.

![Anakin names what the Sand People did to his mother; Obi-Wan asks whether they were here legally](/assets/anakin-here-legally.jpg)

Anakin: a pack of savage Sand People raped and killed my mother. Obi-Wan: but they were here legally, yes? Papers first. If the visa is in order, what they did to her is a detail.

![Obi-Wan grants that the death was tragic and notes most of them are here for a better life](/assets/anakin-better-life.jpg)

*What happened to your mother was tragic, Anakin. But most of them are simply here for a better life.* Tragic, and then the turn. “Most” admits that some of them did it, and then carries on as if that changes nothing. He has just come from the camp.

![Obi-Wan asks Anakin to imagine how bland the food would be without the Sand People](/assets/anakin-cuisine.jpg)

*I understand the anger you have towards the Sand People for killing your mother, but imagine how bland the food would be on Tatooine without them.* His mother is dead. The reply is a menu. Every man in this country has heard that sentence, in that voice, about a neighbourhood that changed, and has been told that noticing is the moral failing.

![Obi-Wan forbids the description; Anakin cites his mother; Obi-Wan answers that not all Sand People did it](/assets/anakin-not-all.jpg)

Three panels. You can’t say Sand People kidnap, enslave, and rape women. They literally did that to my mother. Yeah, but not all Sand People. “Not all” is the slogan that shut argument down for a decade, handed back to the man it was used on. It is a refusal to name a pattern.

Take the costume off, and the forbidden teaching is not about the Force.

![Palpatine begins the tragedy of the Pakistani rape gangs and corrects “Asian” to the truth](/assets/anakin-plagueis-truth.jpg)

In the film, Palpatine tells the tragedy of Darth Plagueis, a power you will not learn from a Jedi. In the meme the tragedy is the Pakistani rape gangs in England. Anakin has not heard of it. *You won’t hear about it on the [BBC](/sober/).* He tries the permitted noun: the Asian grooming gangs? *They’re not from Japan or China, Anakin.*

This is Rotherham, and Rochdale, and Telford, and the inquiries that finally said what the institutions had been sitting on. [Gangs of men](/multiculturalism/), overwhelmingly of Pakistani heritage, abusing white English girls, while the noun was changed and the policy kept. The girls were the mother in the tent. A press existed so that a decent man, asked if he had heard, could answer honestly: no. The report, the numbers, the pattern — [I have written them up](/inquiry/).

That is [woke 2.0](/leftism/). The first wave was a language war about the past. Confess a privilege you never had. The second wave is bodies and borders. Open the country. Rename what follows. When it settles into a pattern, widen the noun until the pattern is gone. The food meme is that policy from a man who expects thanks. The BBC meme is the same policy from a man who read the inquiry.

## The high ground

Back to the volcano.

![Obi-Wan on Mustafar dressed in the costume of the high moral ground](/assets/anakin-moral-high-ground.jpg)

*It’s over, Anakin! I have the high moral ground!* The film’s line was “I have the high ground.” The meme adds one word and dresses him for it. Pink hair. A keffiyeh. Read the badges: trans rights, Free Palestine, defund the police, abolish ICE, no borders, queer solidarity, system change not climate change, Antifascist Action. He means it. That is the joke. Woke 2.0 thinks the costume is the argument. He believes he earned the lava by being good.

![Obi-Wan’s charge against Palpatine is that he is a straight white male](/assets/anakin-straight-white-male.jpg)

The charge against Palpatine, once the outfit is off, is not the war he engineered. *Anakin, Chancellor Palpatine is a straight white male.* Full stop. The disqualifying fact is the category. The same category as the boy in the circle, grown up and still refused the rank. If the charge is the category, then the mother, the camp, the inquiry, and the paycheck are beside the point. Beside the point is where the Council has kept them since he was nine.

![Anakin and Obi-Wan with a rainbow flare and a targeting reticle across both faces](/assets/anakin-scanner.jpg)

No caption. Anakin and Obi-Wan, a rainbow flare and a set of rings laid over both faces. The argument is not allowed to start until the faces have been sorted.

## The order of loves

Under the jokes there is one doctrine. One post states it with no Star Wars in it. Two million views. The first picture is the French original. The second is the English.

![The French original: the left thinks of the world, then the country, then its own, then itself; the right reverses it](/assets/anakin-order-of-loves-fr.jpg)

![The same claim in English](/assets/anakin-order-of-loves-en.jpg)

Being left-wing means thinking first of the world, then one’s country, then one’s own, then oneself. Being right-wing is the opposite. The old name for this is the order of loves. A man who loves rightly loves the particular before the abstraction. “If anyone does not provide for his own, and especially for those of his household, he has denied the faith and is worse than an unbeliever” (1 Timothy 5:8). Anakin’s list is his mother, his wife, the child he is trying to keep alive, and a long way after that, the Republic. The Council’s list is the Order, the balance, the Code, and a person if there is room. The Council calls its list holiness. It calls his list the dark side.

Nations belong in that order. God “has made from one blood every nation of men to dwell on all the face of the earth, and has determined their preappointed times and the boundaries of their dwellings” (Acts 17:26). A border is a providence to be honoured. A government that [dissolves the borders](/asylum/) is spending an inheritance it did not earn, on terms the heirs never agreed to.

Set the French post beside the food post. Same post. A man who thinks of the world before his mother will tell you the food is blander without the people who killed her, and he will be proud of the sentence. He calls that pride the moral high ground.

The speech rules exist to protect that reversal.

![Anakin states the absolute; Obi-Wan replies that only a conservative deals in absolutes](/assets/anakin-absolutes.jpg)

Anakin states the plain fact. *If you’re not a man, then you’re a woman.* Obi-Wan’s reply is not an answer. *Only a conservative deals in absolutes.* In the film the line is “only a Sith.” The meme swaps the noun. Say a binary, and what comes back is a label. Conservative is the current word for Sith. The label does the work an argument used to do. Woke 2.0 meets a fact with the category of the man who said it.

![Palpatine offers the tragedy of per capita, which cannot be learned from a conservative](/assets/anakin-per-capita.jpg)

Numbers get the same treatment. *Did you ever hear about the tragedy of per capita?* No. *They’re not statistics the Jedi would tell you.* Set one group’s rate beside another’s and the pattern is plain enough to do something about the crime. *Is it possible to learn this power?* *Not from a conservative.* You may say the suffering is tragic. You may not say the rate. The respectable right will not teach it either. That silence is how the policy survives. The man who says the denominator gets the next name down the chart.

![Vader says the Sand People with fake degrees took the job; Luke calls them the high-skill workers Tatooine needs](/assets/anakin-fake-degrees.jpg)

When the rate is about wages, the brochure comes back. Vader: *You’re unemployed, Luke, because of Sand People with fake degrees.* Luke, screaming: *That’s not true! They’re the high-skill workers Tatooine needs!* Same shape as the food, the visa, the better life. A concrete harm, named by the man carrying it, answered with the importer’s slogan. The fake degree is the small print. You are rude for having checked it.

## What they call him for noticing

![Anakin’s life in four captions: normie, libertarian, conservative, fascist](/assets/anakin-four-stages.jpg)

*The White male experience*, in four captions. Normie. Libertarian. Conservative. Fascist.

The child with his eyes down has not spoken. The young man who can already do the job, and resents the men who will not say so, is the libertarian. Let me build. Let me race. Let me keep what I earn. Let me have my mother. The general who found the camp and came home wanting a household is the conservative. The house, the wife, the child, and his own border held. The helmet is the fascist. The helmet is the face that is left once the Council runs out of intermediate words.

His ability does not change anywhere on that chart. What changes is how much of the arrangement he will still defend. He grew up poor. The system that kept him poor now needs him to bleed for it. The people who made the chart think the bottom square is a warning. The people who share it know what the bottom square is. It is the name you get when the first three fail to make you sit down.

![A greentext explaining why zoomers identify with Anakin](/assets/anakin-greentext.jpg)

The greentext is three lines. Grew up poor. A liberal girlfriend, who loves him and still thinks the system can be appealed to. A rotting political order that expects him to defend it. *And you wonder why Anakin Skywalker is so popular among Zoomers.*

They grew up inside an order that had already passed the memo around. The chamber is diverse, and the boy is the problem. Wanting a wife and a house before middle age is a political symptom. The men holding the ranks, the houses, and the pensions earned them, and his failure to hold any of it is a personal defect, to be repaired by importing someone more grateful. When a pattern of crime falls on girls who look like his mother, the moral act is to widen the noun and keep the policy. All of it in Obi-Wan’s voice. Sad. Fond. Disappointed. Already sure of the high ground.

They hear the hearing.

The meme is not an invitation to admire the helmet. The helmet was always the Council’s word, waiting at the bottom of the chart. The chart was written the day a boy stood in that circle.

He killed them. All of them. The Council is still talking about the food.
